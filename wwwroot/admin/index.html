Energy tariff data

RegionSample postcodeDual fuelElectricity onlyGas onlyAssumed usageLearned postcodes
How the tariff data works. SmartCubeTariffs.exe (D:\SmartCubeMobile\SmartCubeTariffs) drives energylinx.co.uk once per region and uploads the results here. Three passes are needed because EnergyLinx offers a different list of tariffs for each journey, although the unit rates are identical where a tariff appears in more than one list: dual fuel (about 108 tariffs per region), electricity only (about 106) and gas only (about 61). The app shows dual fuel by default, electricity only when the user has no gas bills or gas supplier, and the user can switch between all three on the tariff page.
To refresh everything: SmartCubeTariffs.exe --auto --fuel all (about 20 minutes, unattended). One fuel: --fuel dual, --fuel elec or --fuel gas; one region: add --region 16. The exe's radio buttons do the same by hand.
Notes: each region's sample address is in D:\SmartCubeMobile\Data\Tarrifs\regions.json and must be one EnergyLinx already knows (a house on an established street); an unknown address makes the site demand meter numbers and the pass fails. "Assumed usage" is whatever EnergyLinx last had for the sample address and only affects its own headline cost column; the app prices every tariff from unit rates and standing charges with the user's own kWh.
